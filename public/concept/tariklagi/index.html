Why we pull it long

  1. Strong tea, condensed milk, one hot mug.
  2. Every pull mixes the milk through.
  3. Every pull cools it towards drinking heat.
  4. The long, high pulls trap air. That is the foam.
  5. Tarik lagi means pull again.
