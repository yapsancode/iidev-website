Your first visit

Nothing happens in the chair until you have heard the plan and the price, and said yes.

  1. Talk

    Tell us what hurts and what worries you. We ask before we look.

  2. Look

    A gentle check, with an X-ray only if we need one. We say what we see as we go.

  3. Plan

    You get your options in writing, each with its price. You choose, today or another day.

Common questions

Will it hurt?

We numb the area first and wait until it has worked. Raise your hand at any time and we stop, every time.

I have not seen a dentist in years. Is that a problem?

No. Many of our patients come back after a long gap. We start with a check-up, tell you what we see, and you decide what to do first.

Do I need an appointment?

A booking keeps your wait short. You can also walk in before 9pm, and we see you when a chair is free.

What if I have a toothache right now?

Message us on WhatsApp. We keep time each evening for people in pain, and we will tell you the earliest we can see you.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, for braces and for any treatment above RM 1,000. You pay monthly with no interest, and the full amount is agreed in writing first.

Can I claim from insurance or my company?

We are not on any panel. You pay us, and we give you an itemised receipt and a dentist’s report that you can submit for your claim.

Do you see children?

Yes, from age 3. The first visit is a look around and a ride in the chair, so the second visit is easy.

Toothache after work?

Message us before 9pm and we will tell you the earliest time we can see you this evening.
